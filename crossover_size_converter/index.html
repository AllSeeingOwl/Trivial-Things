Units

How to measure
Bust / chest
Around the fullest part, tape level across your back.
Underbust
Snug around your ribcage, directly under the bust. Only needed for a cup size.
Waist
Around the narrowest part, usually just above your belly button.
Hips
Around the fullest part of your hips and seat, feet together.
Foot length
Stand on paper, heel against a wall, and measure to the tip of your longest toe. Use the longer foot.
Torso girth
Start on top of one shoulder, go down the front, between your legs, and back up to the same shoulder. This decides whether a one-piece is long enough.
