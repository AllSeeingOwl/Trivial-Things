How to play

  1. Pick a card above. Every card is a random draw from the deck, so friends watching the same thing get different cards.
  2. Tap a square when it happens. Squares glowing orange would finish a line.
  3. Close enough counts. If it’s clearly the same cliché in different words, dab it. Squares with a ≈ list examples: press and hold one, hover over it, or press ? on a keyboard to see them.
  4. Five in a row, column or diagonal is a line. All 25 is a full house, and it means nothing surprised you at all.
  5. Copy card link sends your exact card to a friend. Share result copies an emoji grid of how it went.
  6. Edit squares lists every square in the deck. Swap one off your card, remove ones you never see, or add your own.
