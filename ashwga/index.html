How to play

  1. Pick a card above. Every card is a random draw from the deck, so friends watching the same thing get different cards.
  2. Tap a square when it happens. Squares glowing orange would finish a line.
  3. Five in a row, column or diagonal is a line. All 25 is a full house, and it means nothing surprised you at all.
  4. Copy card link sends your exact card to a friend. Share result copies an emoji grid of how it went.

Make your own card

Got a sport commentator, a podcast or a family dinner that always goes the same way? Write the squares, one per line. You need at least 24.
